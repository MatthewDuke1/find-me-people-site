⚡

Auto-scans every page

The moment a page loads, the extension scans for emails, phone numbers, and contact pages — quietly, in the background. A badge on the icon tells you how many it found.

★

Ranks by likely-support

support@, help@, care@ score green. Marketing, careers, and noreply addresses get filtered to the bottom. Phone numbers near “customer service” or “contact us” get a boost.

Side panel on every page

A discreet pull-tab on the right edge of any page where contacts were found. Click to expand a panel with the same ranked emails and phones, a history of what you’ve copied, business hours, and the support pages worth visiting next.

➤

Compose & Call in one click

Pre-filled email templates for refunds, complaints, cancellations, billing, and support — opened in Gmail, Outlook, or your default client. Phone numbers deep-link into WhatsApp, Google Voice, FaceTime, Teams, or your dialer.

🤖

Sees what chatbots hide New

Detects Intercom, Zendesk, Drift, Crisp, HubSpot, Tidio, LiveChat, Tawk, Freshchat, and Olark widgets. Reads the chatbot’s own help-center articles via its public search API to surface the real support email the bot was trained to deflect from. No chat-UI dance.

🔍

Deeper scanning

Reads hydration state (__NEXT_DATA__, JSON-LD, schema.org) for contacts hidden in JS, scans same-origin iframes, falls back to fetching common contact pages when the visible page is sparse, and requires real contact-context proximity before surfacing a phone (no random snippet numbers from Google search).

⏳

Shows your refund deadline

Enter a charge date on an order or receipt page and Sula counts down every window that applies: the Fair Credit Billing Act (60 days for credit cards), a card chargeback (commonly ~120 days), and the merchant’s own return policy. Missing the deadline is the top reason refunds fail. Free.

Drafts the letter that gets your money back Pro

Seven refund scenarios — unauthorized or duplicate charge, defective, not as described, free-trial auto-renewal, cancelled-but-charged, price drop — each drafted with the right leverage (FCBA, the merchant’s own policy, chargeback, FTC negative-option, CFPB). Real facts fill in; anything Sula doesn’t know stays a blank for you. You hit send.

Tracks & cancels subscriptions Pro

Common questions.

Short, direct answers.

Is Sula free?

Yes for the core. Finding customer-service contacts, seeing your refund deadline, reading a merchant’s own refund policy, and sending the Global Privacy Control opt-out signal are free and stay free. Sula Pro ($6/mo, $57.60/yr, or $80 once) adds refund and complaint letter drafting, chargeback guidance, subscription tracking, the Privacy Guard dashboard, and contact export. The code is open source under the MIT license at github.com/MatthewDuke1/find-me-people, and there are no ads either way.

Does the Global Privacy Control signal actually do anything?

Yes — it’s legally binding, not a courtesy. GPC is the technical way to exercise the opt-out right that already exists under CCPA/CPRA and the privacy laws of roughly a dozen states, including Texas. When Sula sends it, a business covered by those laws is legally required to stop selling or sharing your personal information. Enforcement is real: California fined Sephora $1.2M in 2022 for ignoring GPC signals, and California, Colorado, and Connecticut have run a joint sweep against businesses that don’t honor it. Sula sends the signal two ways — the Sec-GPC request header and the navigator.globalPrivacyControl browser property — on every page, entirely on your device. It can’t force a rogue site to comply, but for a covered business, ignoring it is now a liability.

How do I get a refund with Sula?

Open the order or receipt page. Sula shows how long you have under each remedy — the Fair Credit Billing Act (60 days for credit cards), a card chargeback (commonly around 120 days), and the merchant’s own return window — reads the merchant’s stated policy, and drafts a firm refund letter citing the right leverage. You review it and hit send. Sula takes no cut of your refund. Full refund guide.

How do I dispute a charge or file a chargeback?

Sula checks whether a chargeback is the right move yet — often it tells you to try the merchant in writing first — then gives the exact dispute steps for your card issuer (Chase, Amex, Bank of America, Citi, Capital One, or Wells Fargo). If the bank won’t help, it points you to the CFPB. This guidance is part of Sula Pro. Full dispute guide.

How do I cancel a subscription and stop getting charged?

Sula’s Subscription Guardian tracks your renewal dates and warns you before a free trial converts, and drafts a cancellation letter you can send. If a company keeps charging after you cancelled, that is strong grounds for a card dispute, and Sula helps you file one. Full cancellation guide.

Is Sula like Rocket Money or DoNotPay?

Sula drafts, finds, and tracks, but you always hit send yourself, and it never takes a percentage of your refund or acts as your legal agent. It is a flat $6/mo, not a cut of what you recover. You stay in control of every message that goes out in your name.

Does Sula collect any of my data?

There is no analytics, no tracking, and no server we operate. Core scanning runs on your device. The only network calls are ones you trigger: an optional email MX check that sends a domain (not your full address) to Cloudflare’s public DNS, and — only if you buy Pro — a license check and checkout handled by LemonSqueezy. Autofill details stay on your device and are never transmitted. The full privacy policy walks through every byte the extension touches.

What browsers does Sula work on?

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera, and Firefox 121 and later. Chrome-family browsers install from the Chrome Web Store. Firefox installs from Mozilla Add-ons. All versions ship the same scanner, ranking heuristics, and side panel.

Does Sula work on sites where I’m logged in?

Yes. The extension reads only the public DOM of the page you have open, with the same access your browser already has. It does not transmit page contents anywhere. It also filters out your own logged-in identity (the email visible in account dropdowns) so you do not see your own Gmail address in the results.

How is Sula different from just Googling for a phone number?

Googling routes you to the company’s own help-center page, which is usually where the chatbot lives. Sula reads the underlying contact data the chatbot is gating — schema.org markup, JSON-LD, hydration state, and the chatbot vendor’s public help-center articles — and surfaces the real support email or phone number in seconds. No chat-UI dance.

How do I find a company’s real customer service email instead of a chatbot?

Install Sula and open the company’s website. The extension scans the page — including the help-center data chatbots are trained on — and lists every email it finds, ranked so the address most likely to reach a person sits on top. Click to copy it. No account, and nothing you browse leaves your machine.

How can I find contact info on any website automatically?

Sula scans every page as it loads, right in your browser. A badge on the toolbar icon counts the emails and phone numbers it found, and the popup shows them ranked with copy, compose, and call shortcuts. It works on any site — airlines, banks, stores — because it reads the page itself instead of a database.
