Frequently asked

Is Sula really free?
Yes, for the core. Finding customer-service contacts, seeing your refund deadline, reading a merchant's refund policy, and sending the Global Privacy Control opt-out signal are free and stay free. Sula Pro ($6/mo, $57.60/yr, or $80 once) adds refund and complaint letter drafting, chargeback guidance, subscription tracking, the Privacy Guard dashboard, statement import, and contact export.
What does Sula do with my data?
Nothing leaves your device unless you trigger it. There's no account, no analytics, and no server we operate for the core features. Scanning runs locally in your browser. The only network calls are ones you start — an optional email-domain check, and (only if you buy Pro) a license check handled by LemonSqueezy. See the full privacy policy.
How does Pro / billing work?
Pro is a one-time license key from LemonSqueezy (our payment processor and merchant of record). You buy on their secure checkout, get a key, and activate it in the extension. Manage or cancel a subscription from the receipt email LemonSqueezy sends. Billing questions? Use the form above and pick "Billing or Pro question."
Does Sula ever contact companies for me?
No. Sula finds the contact, drafts the letter, and shows you the steps — but you always hit send yourself. It never acts as your agent, and it never takes a percentage of any refund you recover.
How do I cancel or get a refund on Pro?
Your LemonSqueezy receipt email has a link to manage your subscription and request a refund. If you can't find it, send us a message above with the email you purchased with and we'll sort it out.
How do I uninstall Sula?
Right-click the Sula icon in your toolbar and choose "Remove from Chrome" (or manage extensions in Firefox). Nothing is stored off your device, so removing it deletes everything. We'd love to know why on the way out — the uninstall page takes ten seconds.
