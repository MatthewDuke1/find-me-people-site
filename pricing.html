Questions

I installed Sula before it cost anything. Do I have to pay now?

No. If you had Sula before Pro existed, you keep Pro for free, permanently, with no licence key and nothing to do. You were here first and that counts.

Does Pro change the privacy promise?

No. Scanning still happens entirely on your machine, and contacts still never leave your browser. Save to CRM sends data to your webhook, because you told it to and you chose the destination. Nothing goes to me. See the privacy page.

How many devices?

Three per licence. If you need to move to a new machine, hit Deactivate in the popup on the old one and the slot frees up immediately.

What happens if I cancel?

Pro switches off and Sula goes back to being the free extension. Nothing you already exported disappears, and the scanner keeps working. Lifetime never expires.

Why is lifetime cheaper than it should be?

Because I would rather have you as a user than squeeze you for a subscription. Sula is built by one person, it runs entirely on your machine, and there is no server for me to keep paying for. A one-time price is honest about that.

Ask and you get one. Email sula.support@trysula.com.
